# PR19.5: Progress Story UI Enhancement

**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE  
**Date:** November 4, 2025  
**Type:** Frontend Enhancement (PR19 Extension)

---

## 🎯 Overview

Added a **Spotify Wrapped-style** user-facing entry point for Progress Reels directly on the Overview screen. This transforms the notification-only reel experience into a discoverable, engaging feature that users actively seek out.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

**Inspiration:** Spotify's "Wrapped" year-end experience
- **Prominent placement** on main Overview screen
- **Beautiful gradients** and modern card design
- **Emotional engagement** through emojis and quality scores
- **Effortless navigation** to full reel history

## 🎨 Design Details

### Color Palette
- **Primary gradient:** `#667eea` → `#764ba2` (purple)
- **High quality (90+):** `#f093fb` → `#f5576c` (pink)
- **Medium quality (80-89):** `#667eea` → `#764ba2` (purple)
- **Good quality (<80):** `#4facfe` → `#00f2fe` (blue)
- **Quality badges:** Green (#4CAF50), Orange (#FF9800), Blue (#2196F3)

### Typography
- **Card title:** 20px, bold, white with text shadow
- **Preview text:** 15px, white, 2-line truncation
- **Quality score:** 16px, bold, white
- **History count:** 32px, bold, white (hero section)

### Spacing
- **Card width:** Screen width - 32px (16px margins)
- **Card height:** 220px (optimal for thumb reach)
- **Gallery columns:** 2 (responsive)
- **Grid spacing:** 16px between cards

### Animations
- **Card tap:** `activeOpacity={0.9}` (subtle feedback)
- **Pull-to-refresh:** Native iOS/Android spinner
- **Loading state:** Activity indicator in card center
